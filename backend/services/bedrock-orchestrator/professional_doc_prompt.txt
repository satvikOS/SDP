SECTION 9: GLOSSARY (Alphabetical, 20-30 terms)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Define key terms used throughout:

**CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate):** The rate of return that would be required for an investment to grow from its beginning balance to its ending balance over a specified time period, assuming profits were reinvested at the end of each period.

**Ecosystem Lock-in:** A competitive moat created when customers/developers/partners become dependent on a platform or standard, making switching prohibitively expensive in terms of time, money, or compatibility. Example: 90% of defense AI code uses CUDA; rewriting would cost $20B and 5+ years.

**EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization):** A measure of company operating performance. Expressed as percentage of revenue, indicates operational efficiency.

**HHI (Herfindahl-Hirschman Index):** Market concentration measure. Sum of squared market shares. <1500 = competitive, 1500-2500 = moderate concentration, >2500 = highly concentrated.

**Real Option:** A strategic investment that creates future flexibility. Gives management the right (but not obligation) to take future action. Valued using option pricing theory.

**ROIC (Return on Invested Capital):** Net operating profit after tax divided by invested capital. Measures how efficiently a company uses capital to generate profits. >15% typically considered good, >25% excellent.

**Stranded Assets:** Investments that lose economic value before end of useful life due to technology shifts, regulatory changes, or market disruption. Example: coal plants shut down before debt is repaid.

**Value Migration:** The shift of profit pools within an industry value chain. Example: Defense value migrating from hardware (90%) to software/intelligence (90%) over 20 years.
